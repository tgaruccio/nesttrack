The part nobody enjoys

Better to know now

Almost every problem that blows up a purchase was findable at the start. Credit, income documentation, a debt someone forgot about — none of it is unusual, and none of it is fatal when there is time to deal with it.

What makes it painful is finding out in the middle of a transaction, with a seller waiting and a closing date on the calendar. That is the only real argument for doing this before you start looking rather than after you have found the house.
